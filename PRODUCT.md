# CatFit (FitTrack)

Personal fitness PWA for Thai users — workout planning, nutrition logging, health PDF insights, and body tracking. Local-first, mobile-first, used between gym sets and daily life.

**Register:** product  
**Audience:** Adults tracking fitness and health checkups in Thailand  
**Mood:** Energetic, confident, approachable — not clinical  
**Brand colors:** Yellow + black (high contrast, active)
